# Automation consumer contract

Use after the role's prescribed first command has started, or for a returned
repair event. A lock/no-op stop takes precedence over further context loading.

## Ownership and evidence

- Match the exact initial scheduler Automation ID and retain its actual Thread.
  Bind an absent environment ID only from that header; a foreign inherited ID
  stops before effects. A title, cwd, branch or copied prompt is not ownership.
- Retain one original process/stdin, run/date/phase, immutable inputs and absolute
  budget. On an exact duplicate-owner or no-op gate, follow that gate and finish.
  Why: resource guards protect economic scope; unrelated tasks are not peer locks.
- Consume stage events and exact cited artifacts. Verify raw/canonical hashes,
  count, date, run and original checkpoint where that consumer requires them.
  A report, ready bundle, navigation or delivered notice proves only its own stage.
- The source code implements behavior; Operating Contract defines authority.
  A mismatch is a repair issue, not permission to bypass a real guard. Dated
  reviews explain historical choices; they do not supersede current rules.

## Repair loop and completion

Use Operating Contract §1e and [book-b-live-repair.md](book-b-live-repair.md):
inspect exact failure → classify actual current dependency/scope → repair or
prove isolation → minimum correctness validation → legal original continuation
→ terminal effects/receipt. Keep inherited audit state separate from current
cash/exposure/code effects. Missing proof can pause affected new BUY while the
owner restores it; protective SELL keeps its own owned/sellable/market guards.
Why: an irreversible window cannot be recreated after a premature task closeout.

A process terminal is not task completion when authorized local recovery remains.
Before a one-time continuation, show what dependency changed. Preserve claims,
original deadlines and consumed continuation fences; never chain a budget,
replace an active runner, replay a terminal batch or retry an uncertain write.
Only an actual credential/permission/consent/external-evidence decision beyond
available authority requires the user. Do not convert a missing recovery port
into a claim that economic trading is impossible or that the repair is complete.

For APP failure follow native_readback_evidence path/hash; retained bad cells
are diagnostic_only, not an admitted account snapshot or a recreated old read.
Engineering APP tests obey AGENTS.md and the app_test_window_status.py gate;
production repair/reconciliation is separate. After a change, complete the
independent Standards/Spec review and scoped validation; report deferred tests.

## Financial, strategy and notification boundaries

- [Strategy consumption](../../../../docs/STRATEGY_CONSUMPTION.md) routes current
  policy vs legacy/research features. Contract §2a/§4/§5/§6/§9 govern execution.
  Ordinary COLD stays blocked; only the validated cited v2 Xiaocao explicit
  mode-follow exception can restore COLD inside original frozen candidates.
  UNKNOWN mode, account/market/risk and exact-once gates remain binding.
- Fresh current APP cash/owned marks and qualified risk proof are distinct from
  final accounting. Consume account.accounting and observation/journal/funding
  hashes. Funding is not profit, frozen cash is not loss, incomplete actual fees
  or unclassified cash keep final PnL N/A. Do not classify cash, reset high water,
  mint capital authority, absorb manual holdings or resize an existing intent.
- Paper A/B/T, APP and authority=0 funded accounts keep separate writers/fills.
  Research cannot prove APP fills. Only the task's authorized post-business
  acquisition runs, once, after original business terminals; a gate stop has none.
- Preflight start/ready/recoverable problems are local audit. Consolidate current
  orders/fills/cause and concrete user actions; historical routine gaps stay local.
  Delivered/uncertain claims never resend for wording changes. Retry only proved
  pre-send failures through the established outboxes after business.
- Summaries are compact Chinese: result/cause/user action, orders/fills, fresh
  cash/NAV and support/latency only when proved. Missing terminal is unproved,
  not zero fills. Keep technical payloads in artifacts. Delivery is not human ACK.
