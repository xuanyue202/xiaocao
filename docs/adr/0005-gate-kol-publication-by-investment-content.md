# Promote source items only after the KOL investment-content gate

A captured source item becomes a KOL publication event only when complete coverage review finds at least one attributable investment-decision claim. Items with no such claim stop at a durable low-density audit terminal and create neither a 灰常亮 report nor an Enterprise WeChat alert. Promoted events always create a complete 灰常亮 report; events with current market posture, buy/sell/hold action, position boundary, direction choice, or actionable trigger also receive one concise Enterprise WeChat alert. For information-rich live sessions this alert gate is intentionally permissive and does not require a reversal from the previous session, while historical initialization, expired intraday commentary, pure confirmation, and methodology-only material may remain report-only. This keeps weak fragments from cluttering the reader surface without suppressing useful conditional or hold/no-trade signals.
